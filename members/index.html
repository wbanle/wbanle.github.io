路利军（Lijun Lu）Principal Investigator

Prof. Lu is currently a professor at the School of Biomedical Engineering, Southern Medical University, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China, and serves as a Ph.D. supervisor and Director of the Department of Medical Engineering. He is a recipient of the Pearl River Talented Young Investigator award of Guangdong Province and was a visiting scholar at Johns Hopkins University, USA. His main research interests include PET imaging and image analysis. Prof. Lu has led or completed 4 projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC), 1 project under the National Key R&D Program of the Ministry of Science and Technology of China, 1 Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Program international cooperation project, 4 projects funded by the Natural Science Foundation of Guangdong Province, 1 Doctoral Program Fund project of the Ministry of Education, 1 Guangdong Provincial Quality Engineering university–industry joint laboratory project, and 1 Guangdong Provincial postgraduate model course. He has published over 60 SCI-indexed papers as the first or corresponding author in leading medical imaging journals, including Medical Image Analysis (MedIA), European Journal of Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging (EJNMMI), IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (IEEE JBHI), Radiology: Artificial Intelligence, European Radiology, Molecular Imaging and Biology, Physics in Medicine & Biology, and Medical Physics. He holds 10 authorized Chinese invention patents. Dr. Lu serves as a reviewer for more than ten journals, including IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, European Radiology, and Physics in Medicine & Biology. He also serves as a committee member of the Youth Working Committee of the Chinese Society of Biomedical Engineering, a committee member of the Medical Image Information and Control Branch, a committee member of the Intelligent Imaging Branch of the Chinese Society for Stereology, and Chair of the Intelligent Medical Imaging Branch of the Guangdong Society of Biomedical Engineering.

吕闻冰（Wenbin Lv）

云南大学信息学院副教授，云南大学信息学院信息与电子工程系主任。博士毕业于南方医科大学生物医学工程学院，加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚大学访问学者，入选云南大学青年人才培优计划，获得云南省“兴滇英才支持计划”青年人才称号。研究方向为肿瘤医学图像分析与临床应用，长期致力于多模态医学图像智能诊疗关键技术研究。在领域权威期刊发表论文20余篇，Google Scholar引用1098次，H-index=15，单篇最高引用166次，引用量60次以上论文10篇。主持国自然青年项目1项，博士后面上项目1项，人才启动项目1项，省市校级项目6项。研究方向为：PET/CT、MRI影像鼻咽癌、头颈癌诊断与预后建模；鼻咽癌EBV DNA动态风险预测；CT剂量图像放射性肺炎预测；PET双示踪剂图像分离、生成；结直肠内镜图像分级；乳腺容积超声分割、分类；CT眼科肿瘤病理分型；CT急性胰腺炎分割。

杜东阳（Dongyang Du）

内蒙古大学计算机学院讲师。2016–2023年在南方医科大学获生物医学工程博士学位，期间赴加拿大不列颠哥伦比亚大学癌症研究中心访学。研究方向为医学图像分析、PET/CT影像组学及机器学习，聚焦肺癌诊断与预后模型构建。以第一作者发表多篇SCI论文，参与多项国家自然科学基金项目，并主持内蒙古自然科学基金青年基金项目一项，高层次人才科研启动金项目1项。2025年被认定为内蒙古自治区“英才兴蒙”工程高层次人才第六类。
